The 7th day of Novmber, 2026

My Dearest Keyne,

I have taken up my keyboard which I will call a pen this time to feel like a victorian this evening I have sat down and wrote my love to you once again mehehehe. I just wanted to say I am so happy that I have seen you smile more than ever from the day that we've met. The smile that brightens up my mood to the point of make me forget everything that is weighing me down

You are the quiet hour I look forward to everyday. When the world grows loud, it is your voice I listen for. I keep the small things you say the way others keep pressed flowers between the pages of a favourite book.

The longer the days I've spent with you the deeper the my love grows deep within me, I want to thank you for choosing this life with me and counquering our battles of time and distance that we face together.

My hardworking, gentle, and loving future Mrs. Daguil Thank you so much for being you and choosing what's best for your life. Just know that when you are happy I am happy.

Still, every laugh and smile you show me is a gift that I treasure deeply. I love it when your eyes disappear when you smile and laugh. I really love each and every aspect that you have so much that I want it for the rest of my life.

November 7th and we're staying strong with less to no conflicts and still as sweet as ever. Once again, Thank you for being my everything.

❧

Ever and always thine,

Your Devoted Heart

P.S. You broke all five seals yourself, which seems only right, since you have held the key to my heart for quite some time.
